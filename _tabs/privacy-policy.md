Welcome to K-Unpacked. We respect your privacy, and this page explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you visit this site, who collects it, and what choices you have.

---

## 1. Who We Are

K-Unpacked (kunpacked.com) is an independent blog covering K-Pop, K-Drama, K-Movie, and Korean culture. We do not require you to create an account or log in to read anything on this site.

---

## 2. Information Collected Automatically

We do not directly ask for or store your name, email address, or other personal contact information. However, like almost every website on the internet, K-Unpacked and the third-party services it relies on (described below) automatically collect certain technical data whenever you visit, including:

- Your IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country or city)
- Browser type, device type, and operating system
- Pages visited, time spent on each page, and referring/exit pages
- Cookies and similar identifiers (described in Section 5)
- Web beacons or similar pixel technology, which may be used by analytics and advertising partners to measure ad delivery and page activity

This information is collected in aggregate and is not used by us to identify you personally.

---

## 3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (which pages are popular, how long people stay, and general traffic trends). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to collect the technical data listed in Section 2.

Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
Opt out: [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

## 4. Advertising — Google AdSense and Third-Party Vendors

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and, potentially, other third-party advertising vendors. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and other identifiers (including your IP address) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet ("interest-based" or "personalized" advertising).

**Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.** Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

- [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads)
- [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) (opt out of participating third-party vendors)
- [www.networkadvertising.org](https://www.networkadvertising.org/choices/) (Network Advertising Initiative opt-out)

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services: [Google Ads Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

### Consent for Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, this site (through its advertising and analytics partners) is designed to request your consent before setting non-essential cookies or using your data for personalized advertising, in line with applicable consent requirements. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through the consent management options presented on this site, or through your browser and device settings.

## 5. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. K-Unpacked itself does not set cookies to track you directly, but the third-party services described above (Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and any other embedded content such as YouTube search links) may set their own cookies when you visit.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of this site function, but the site will remain readable without them.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own database of visitor personal information, so we do not retain personal data beyond what our third-party analytics and advertising providers retain under their own policies (linked above). For details on how long Google retains data collected through Analytics or AdSense, see [Google's data retention documentation](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

## 7. External Links

Our posts may include links to YouTube search results, Netflix, news outlets, and other external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those external sites. Please review their own privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

## 8. Children's Privacy

K-Unpacked does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this site or its embedded third-party services, please [contact us](/contact/) so we can address it.

In addition to the opt-out links above, most browsers let you block or delete cookies, and mobile devices offer settings to limit ad tracking. Because these settings vary by browser and device, we recommend checking your specific browser or device's help documentation for exact steps.
