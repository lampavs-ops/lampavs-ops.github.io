## Ryoo Seung-wan — Director

Ryoo Seung-wan is often called "the Action Kid of Korean cinema," a nickname he says he genuinely likes. Born in 1973, he grew up obsessed with martial arts films and started making short movies as a teenager. Before Veteran, he had already built a strong career with movies like *The Unjust* (2010) and *The Berlin File* (2013).

In interviews, Ryoo has said that his two films before Veteran were dark, and the heroes in them always seemed to lose. With Veteran, he wanted to try something different: a hero who actually wins. He also said he could not fully explain why the movie became such a massive hit, but he believed audiences found satisfaction in watching a fictional hero defeat powerful and corrupt people, something that is difficult to see happen in real life.

Ryoo went on to direct the 2024 sequel, *I, the Executioner*, which brought Seo Do-cheol back to the screen nearly a decade later.

---

## Hwang Jung-min as Seo Do-cheol

Hwang Jung-min plays Do-cheol, the loud, relentless detective at the center of the story. Hwang had already worked with director Ryoo Seung-wan on *The Unjust*, and Ryoo has said he wrote the role of Do-cheol with Hwang specifically in mind. According to the director, the character's dialogue and actions matched Hwang's own personality so closely that it felt written for him.

Hwang Jung-min is one of Korea's most respected actors, known for a wide range of roles across action, drama, and even musicals. Hwang led the film as Seo Do-cheol, and Veteran went on to become the highest-grossing Korean film of 2015, selling over 13.4 million tickets.

---

## Yoo Ah-in as Jo Tae-oh

Yoo Ah-in plays Tae-oh, the reckless, cruel heir who becomes Do-cheol's target. Interestingly, Ryoo Seung-wan has said Yoo Ah-in was not his first choice for the role. Many young actors avoid villain roles because they can hurt a commercial, likable image.

The two met socially at the Busan International Film Festival, where Yoo Ah-in showed interest in the project and later asked to read the script. When Ryoo tried to add more explanation for why the character was so evil, Yoo Ah-in reportedly questioned why the character needed such an extensive explanation, asking why he could not simply be evil without justification. Ryoo said he was grateful for that response.

---

## Yoo Hae-jin as Executive Choi

Yoo Hae-jin plays Executive Choi, a senior company executive and Jo Tae-oh's trusted right-hand man. He is one of Korea's most reliable character actors, known for finding both humor and menace in supporting roles across many major Korean films.

---

## Oh Dal-su as Team Leader Oh

Oh Dal-su plays Team Leader Oh, Do-cheol's superior on the police force. Oh Dal-su has spent decades building a career as one of Korea's most familiar character actors, with a background rooted in theater in Busan. The same year Veteran was released, he also earned major acting awards for his role in *Ode to My Father* (2014).

---

## Jang Yoon-ju as Miss Bong

Jang Yoon-ju plays Miss Bong, a sharp detective on Do-cheol's team who also becomes his undercover "wife" in the film's opening sting operation. Before acting, Jang built a career as a fashion model, and she has continued working across both modeling and acting.
