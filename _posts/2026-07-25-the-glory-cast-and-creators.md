If you have followed this series, you already know the story. [Part 1](/posts/the-glory-part1-intro/) introduced *The Glory* and Song Hye-kyo's dramatic image change. [Part 2](/posts/the-glory-part2-deep-dive/) broke down the ending and its biggest twists.

This final part is about the people who built the show. Let's meet the writer, the director, and the cast who made *The Glory* one of the most talked-about K-dramas in recent years.

## The Writer: Kim Eun-sook

Kim Eun-sook is one of the most successful screenwriters in Korean television. Before *The Glory*, she was already known for huge hits like *Descendants of the Sun*, *Goblin*, and *Mr. Sunshine*. Most of her earlier work leaned toward romance and warm, witty dialogue.

*The Glory* was a big departure for her. It is darker, slower, and far more painful than her past projects. Kim has explained that the idea developed after her teenage daughter asked her a striking question: whether it would hurt more to beat someone nearly to death, or to be beaten nearly to death herself. That question became the seed for Dong-eun's story. Kim also studied real Korean school-bullying cases while writing the series.

Kim Eun-sook does not keep a public social media account, so there is no official SNS link to share for her.

## The Director: Ahn Gil-ho

Ahn Gil-ho directed *The Glory*, bringing his experience from tense, atmospheric dramas like *Stranger* and *Memories of the Alhambra*. His style favors quiet tension over big, loud moments, which fit perfectly with a story about a revenge plan built slowly over nearly two decades.

Fans online have often pointed out that pairing Kim Eun-sook's writing with Ahn Gil-ho's directing gave *The Glory* a very different feel from Kim's earlier, more romantic dramas.

Ahn Gil-ho also does not appear to keep a public social media account, so no official SNS link is available for him either.

## Song Hye-kyo as Moon Dong-eun

Song Hye-kyo plays Moon Dong-eun, the woman at the center of the entire revenge plot. As covered in Part 1 of this series, this role marked a major shift for an actress long associated with warm, romantic leads.

## Lee Do-hyun as Joo Yeo-jeong

Lee Do-hyun plays Joo Yeo-jeong, the doctor who becomes Dong-eun's closest ally and, eventually, much more. Lee had already built a strong reputation through dramas like *Sweet Home* and *Youth of May* before this role expanded his range even further.

> 📸 **Official SNS:** [Instagram @ldh_sky](https://www.instagram.com/ldh_sky/)

## Lim Ji-yeon as Park Yeon-jin

Lim Ji-yeon plays Park Yeon-jin, the show's central villain. She already had a solid acting career before this role, including her award-winning film debut in *Obsessed* and a supporting part in *Money Heist: Korea*. But *The Glory* became a major career breakthrough for her, bringing her much wider international recognition.

> 📸 **Official SNS:** [Instagram @limjjy2](https://www.instagram.com/limjjy2/)

## Jung Sung-il as Ha Do-yeong

Jung Sung-il plays Ha Do-yeong, Yeon-jin's husband. His character's slow realization of the truth about his wife becomes one of the story's quieter but more emotional threads.

## Park Sung-hoon as Jeon Jae-jun

Park Sung-hoon plays Jeon Jae-jun, one of Dong-eun's former bullies. His portrayal of a wealthy, spoiled man slowly losing control of his own life became one of the more talked-about performances from the supporting cast.

> 📸 **Official SNS:** [Instagram @boxabum](https://www.instagram.com/boxabum/)

## Yeom Hye-ran as Kang Hyeon-nam

Yeom Hye-ran plays Kang Hyeon-nam, a woman trapped in an abusive marriage who becomes an unlikely ally to Dong-eun. Yeom is a respected veteran actress, and her performance added real emotional weight to one of the show's more grounded storylines.

> 📸 **Official SNS:** Official account not publicly confirmed.

## Why This Cast Worked So Well Together

*The Glory* did not rely on just one strong performance. It needed an entire ensemble willing to play deeply unlikable, morally complicated characters and make them feel real. From Song Hye-kyo's icy control to Lim Ji-yeon's chilling charm, the cast turned a slow-burn revenge story into something audiences could not look away from.

That combination of a bold script, patient direction, and a fearless cast is exactly why *The Glory* became a global phenomenon, and why many K-drama fans continue to regard it as one of the genre's standout revenge dramas.
