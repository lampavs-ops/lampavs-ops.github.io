A few weeks ago, I wrote about how RESCENE snuck into my life through one YouTube Shorts video. (You can read that first story [right here](/posts/rescene-now-hot-in-korea/).) Back then, I was just a fan watching from my couch. I had no idea what was coming next.

Since then, RESCENE has gone from a niche favorite to one of the most talked-about girl groups in Korea. And the story did not stop at "trending." It kept going, in ways that surprised even me.

## From "Rising" to "Number One"

RESCENE's big summer actually came in two connected steps. On July 8, their 2024 song "LOVE ATTACK" pulled off a huge chart comeback and [hit #1 on Melon's TOP100](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202607091059001), almost two years after it first came out. Around the same time, RESCENE released a cover of KARA's "Pretty Girl." That new song rode the wave straight to [RESCENE's first-ever music show trophy on July 14](https://www.soompi.com/article/1854632wpp/watch-rescene-takes-tearful-1st-ever-win-on-the-show-with-pretty-girl).

I remember watching the clip of that first win. The members could not stop crying. Honestly, so did I a little. Counting their debut day as day one, that trophy came on day 841. That is a long time to wait.

## Minami's Quiet Pause (and a Big Surprise)

Here is something I did not expect. In June, Minami said she wanted to step back from her bubbly "gyaru" character for a while. A few headlines even called it a "retirement." But in [a follow-up interview on July 3](https://www.starnewskorea.com/star/2026/07/03/2026070221451810624), she cleared it up herself: it was not permanent. She just wanted fans to see her "Pretty Girl" singer side first, and said she could always bring gyaru back later.

Then came the real surprise. Minami became known for an "absolute pitch" style test, where she named the exact pitch of random everyday sounds and got them all right. She also speaks Korean so well that hosts on a variety show joked she sounded more polished than people from Seoul, after five years in Korea, much of it self-taught. She has said English felt easier to her when she was young, and today she calls her English good enough for listening and everyday conversation. And she showed off eight years of calligraphy training from Japan, writing elegant letters with total focus.

![RESCENE member practicing calligraphy at a low table, brush and paper, warm lamp light, on black background paper](/assets/img/posts/rescene-after-the-win-calligraphy.jpg)
*A quiet moment can say more than a comedy skit ever could.*

I will be honest. When I first saw the gyaru videos, I thought that was just "her thing." Watching her write calligraphy in total silence made me realize how wrong I was. She is a serious artist who also happens to be funny. That is a rare mix.

## Zena's Story Started in Gyeongju

The next part hit me even harder. [Reporters visited the dance studio in Gyeongju](https://www.idaegu.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=664806), Zena's hometown, where she first learned to move. She started there in 2020, in sixth grade, after her mother encouraged her to try dance while everyone was stuck at home during the pandemic. The studio's director says Zena built her confidence slowly, practicing not just steps but facial expressions too.

The kids training there now do not call Zena a princess. They say they want to become "the second Silla princess" themselves, just like her, a nod to the ancient kingdom that once ruled the area.

My son is close to the age Zena was when she started. Watching that video with him made the whole idol dream feel less like a fairy tale and more like real work that began on one ordinary day.

## Liv Finally Gets Her Moment

For a while, Liv stayed mostly in the background online. That changed fast this summer. [A June episode built around Liv and fellow member May, titled "We're RESCENE too," passed 4.46 million views in just three days](https://v.daum.net/v/20260615102040460). Fans could not stop talking about Liv especially.

What do people love about her? She is a little goofy. She throws herself into jokes with zero fear of looking silly. But she is also warm, the kind of member who checks on the others. Fans also started digging up older photos and clips of her as her visibility grew, and plenty of people were surprised by how much her look has changed over the years.

It is nice to see a member get her flowers a little late, but get them all the same.

## The Stage Got Bigger: RESCENE in LA

In mid-August, [RESCENE flew to Los Angeles for KCON LA 2026](https://www.soompi.com/article/1828181wpp/kcon-la-2026-announces-lineup). A group that used to hand out paper flyers on the street was now performing for fans across the ocean.

I showed my son the clips from LA. He asked, "Wait, they're famous in America now too?" I laughed, but it is a fair question. Their fanbase outside Korea used to be much smaller, even though [GRAMMY.com had already picked out "LOVE ATTACK" as one of the most exciting K-pop songs of 2024](https://www.grammy.com/news/k-pop-songs-that-electrified-2024-riize-kiss-of-life-aespa).

I keep thinking about how strange that jump must feel for them. Just months earlier, they were folding flyers by hand on a street corner. Now they are landing in another country because thousands of strangers decided to care.

## The Old Videos Fans Keep Finding

Here is the part of the RESCENE story that moves me the most. Ever since they blew up, fans have been digging through clips from before their 2026 breakthrough. And what they found is honestly kind of amazing.

Only four months before that Los Angeles stage, in April 2026, [the members were still out on the street themselves, handing out paper flyers to promote a free concert called "Girl Groove."](https://www.starnewskorea.com/music/2026/04/06/2026040610223024598) Older clips also show the group calling on other celebrities to join dance challenges, hoping the extra attention would help people notice RESCENE, plus a few rough live moments, including one where the sound cut out mid-song and the group kept singing and dancing anyway.

This was real, hands-on promotional work, not a big-budget campaign run by a huge team. It was five people trying almost everything they could think of, on a much smaller budget than most major-label groups get.

I think that is why so many fans keep coming back to these old clips. It is not just nostalgia. It is proof. Groups like NewJeans, ILLIT, and aespa debuted under some of the biggest companies in K-pop, with far bigger budgets and marketing teams behind every move. RESCENE debuted under the much smaller The Muze Entertainment. They still had a team producing and promoting them, just on a very different scale.

That difference is exactly why this win feels so earned.

## What Comes Next

RESCENE still has a long road ahead. One win, one big trip, and a handful of viral clips do not erase years of hard work overnight. But something has clearly shifted. Through mid-August, they kept racking up wins, including [a first-ever Triple Crown for "Pretty Girl" on Music Core](https://www.soompi.com/article/1863174wpp/watch-rescene-takes-4th-win-and-1st-ever-triple-crown-for-pretty-girl-on-music-core-performances-by-stray-kids-kiiikiii-and-more). Fans keep multiplying. And the members finally seem to believe, out loud, that this is really happening to them.

I do not know if RESCENE will become the next global giant. Nobody does, not even their own agency. But I know that whatever happens next, I will be watching, because I already know how hard they worked just to get here.
