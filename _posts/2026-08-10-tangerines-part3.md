## Kim Won-seok — Director

Kim Won-seok is one of Korean television's most respected directors, known for a patient, deeply human style that never rushes an emotional moment. Before this project, he directed *My Mister* and *Misaeng*, two dramas already beloved for finding quiet dignity in ordinary, struggling lives, along with the time-crossing crime thriller *Signal*.

Accepting Best Drama at the 61st Baeksang Arts Awards in 2025, Kim said he hoped that, in an age of growing hatred, the series might encourage people not to hate one another and to find a way to live well together. That instinct, to find tenderness inside hardship, runs through everything he directs.

---

## Lim Sang-choon — Writer

Lim Sang-choon wrote the series, drawing on the same gift for warmth and humor inside difficult circumstances that defined her earlier hit, *When the Camellia Blooms*. Her screenplay for this series earned her the Best Screenplay award at the same Baeksang ceremony.

---

## IU as Oh Ae-sun and Yang Geum-myeong

> 📸 **Official SNS:** [Instagram @dlwlrma](https://www.instagram.com/dlwlrma/)

IU carries the emotional core of the entire series in a dual role, playing both Ae-sun in her younger years and, later, Ae-sun's grown daughter, Geum-myeong. Long one of Korea's most successful singers, IU has also built a strong acting career. Her dual performance was widely acclaimed and later earned her Best Actress at the 4th Blue Dragon Series Awards in July 2025, her first win in that category.

---

## Park Bo-gum as Yang Gwan-sik

> 📸 **Official SNS:** [Instagram @bogummy](https://www.instagram.com/bogummy/)

Park Bo-gum plays the younger version of Gwan-sik, the steady, devoted man who has loved Ae-sun since childhood. Known for roles in dramas like *Reply 1988* and *Record of Youth*, Park brings a quiet warmth to Gwan-sik that anchors the entire love story.

---

## Moon So-ri as Older Oh Ae-sun

Moon So-ri takes over as the older Ae-sun, portraying her through the later stages of her life. A veteran, widely respected film actress, she brings decades of weathered emotion to the role.

---

## Park Hae-joon as Older Yang Gwan-sik

Park Hae-joon plays Gwan-sik in the later stages of his life, alongside Moon So-ri. Known for *My Mister*, *The World of the Married*, and *12.12: The Day*, he brings the same steadiness to older Gwan-sik that Park Bo-gum established in the character's younger years.

---

## Yeom Hye-ran as Jeon Gwang-rye

Yeom Hye-ran plays Ae-sun's mother, Gwang-rye, a haenyeo, one of Jeju's traditional female sea divers. Her performance earned her the Best Supporting Actress award at the 61st Baeksang Arts Awards. Yeom is also known for roles in *When the Camellia Blooms*, *The Uncanny Counter*, and *The Glory*.

---

## Choi Dae-hoon as Bu Sang-gil

Choi Dae-hoon plays Bu Sang-gil, who grew up alongside Gwan-sik in Jeju. His performance earned him the Best Supporting Actor award at the same ceremony.

---

## Kim Seon-ho as Park Chung-seob

Kim Seon-ho makes a special appearance as Park Chung-seob, an artist who eventually becomes Geum-myeong's husband. Known for *Start-Up* and *Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha*, Kim brings a gentle warmth to Chung-seob that makes the character's slow-building romance with Geum-myeong land so well.
