## Not Picked One by One. Picked as a Team.

Most K-pop groups get built the same way: trainees compete, some get cut, and only the "winners" debut. Stray Kids did something different.

In 2017, JYP Entertainment launched a reality survival show, also called *Stray Kids*, to introduce a new boy group. But instead of pitting trainees against each other, the show worked as "trainees versus JYP." Nine young trainees were chosen as a set team from the very start, and their real goal was proving to the company, as a group, that all nine of them deserved to debut together.

That team-first idea came from an unusual source. Member **Bang Chan** had already been training at JYP for years and personally helped choose several of his future groupmates before the show even began. In other words, before the cameras rolled, the "captain" was already picking his own crew.

Along the way, two members, Lee Know and Felix, were actually eliminated during the show. But in the final episode, they were both brought back, and all nine trainees debuted together as planned.

## What "Stray Kids" Actually Means

The name is not random. "Stray Kids" is meant to capture the idea of young people who step off the path everyone expects them to follow, kids who "leave home" to search for who they really are, on their own terms.

That theme runs through the group's whole identity. From the very beginning, three members, **Bang Chan, Changbin, and Han**, made up an in-house production team called **3RACHA**. They write and produce a huge portion of Stray Kids' own music, which is rare for a group this big. Most idol groups rely almost entirely on outside songwriters. Stray Kids built their sound from the inside out.

---

## Debut: District 9 and a Dark, Defiant Sound

Stray Kids officially debuted on March 25, 2018, with a showcase in Seoul, followed by their first mini album, *I Am Not*, and its title track, "District 9." Before that, the show itself had already introduced the group's rougher, moodier sound through their pre-debut single "Hellevator," released while the survival show was still airing.

Right from the start, the group leaned into intense, genre-mixing production: heavy bass, industrial synths, and lyrics about pressure, rebellion, and self-worth. It was a bold choice for a rookie group, and it set Stray Kids apart from softer, more polished debuts happening around them.

## The Lineup, Then and Now

Stray Kids debuted with nine members. In October 2019, member Woojin left the group to pursue solo activities, and Stray Kids has continued as an eight-member group ever since:

- **Bang Chan** — leader, producer
- **Lee Know**
- **Changbin** — producer
- **Hyunjin**
- **Han** — producer
- **Felix**
- **Seungmin**
- **I.N**
